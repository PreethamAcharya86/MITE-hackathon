Guidelines

Rules & Regulations

Know the rules before you build — fair play makes the best hackathon.

Team Size

Teams must consist of 2 to 4 members. Solo participants are not allowed. Form your squad wisely!

Time Limit

All coding must happen within the official 6-hour window (9:05 AM – 4:00 PM). No pre-built projects.

AI-Powered Builds

Projects must incorporate AI/ML components. Pure front-end or non-AI solutions will not be evaluated.

Original Work

Plagiarism is strictly prohibited. All code must be original. Open-source libraries must be credited.

Judging Criteria

Projects are judged on Innovation (35%), Technical Complexity (25%), Impact (20%), and Presentation (20%).

Submission

All teams must submit their project demo and GitHub link via the official form before the deadline.
